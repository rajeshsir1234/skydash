How to Play

     
        Advertisement      
     

🎮 Controls

     

        On desktop, press Space or the Up Arrow to make your character fly.         On mobile, tap anywhere on the game area.      

     

⭐ Collect Stars

     

        Stars increase your score. Try to collect as many as possible         while avoiding obstacles.      

     

🚧 Avoid Obstacles

     

        Hitting an obstacle ends the current run.         You can restart the game and try to beat your previous record.      

     

🏆 High Score

     

        Sky Dash stores your best score locally in your browser.         Clearing browser storage may remove your saved score.      

     

Tips

     
           
  • Use short taps instead of holding the button continuously.
    •        
  • Watch the upcoming obstacles carefully.
    •        
  • Do not focus only on the stars; staying alive is more important.
